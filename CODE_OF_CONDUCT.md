# Code of conduct

ContextLens contributors are expected to make participation respectful,
welcoming, and constructive.

Be considerate in technical disagreement. Critique ideas and evidence rather
than people. Do not harass, threaten, insult, discriminate against, or expose
private information about another participant. Respect project maintainers'
decisions about scope, safety, licensing, and community boundaries.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behavior harms the community. Reports should include relevant context and
should not be used for retaliation or bad-faith pressure.

Until a dedicated moderation address is published, report conduct concerns
privately to the repository maintainers. Do not open a public issue containing
personal or sensitive information.
